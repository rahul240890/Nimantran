# Posting the videos

Where each video goes, with its caption, hashtags and title. Links point at pages that work today: the sample invitations at `/sample/<design>` (the guest's view, with "Choose" to start your own) and the home page. Instagram shows at most a few hashtags well, so each caption carries five; YouTube takes more in the description.

## When

India's wedding season runs from after Diwali to February, so October and November are when couples look for invitations. Post in the evening, 7 to 9 pm India time, when people scroll after work; on weekends, late morning works too.
