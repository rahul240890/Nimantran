# Templates 16 to 25: image prompts

For the owner, 17 October 2026. Ten more templates after the first fifteen ([TEMPLATE_PROMPTS.md](TEMPLATE_PROMPTS.md)), each a complete set: **its own gate opening, nine pages and two couple-photo pages, 15 pictures per template (150 in all)**. Every one is a look we don't have yet: none repeats our page themes, Scenes, gates or the first fifteen.

## What's new this time

- **New art in every template.** Bronze cast like Dhokra, paintings behind glass, a Chettinad mansion, Meenakari enamel, Kutch rogan painting, dark old-master florals, pastel symmetry, Old Delhi, hammered silver and hot-air balloons over Rajasthan.
- **New families served.** Tamil (Chettinad), Kutchi (Rogan), Delhi and Punjabi (Purani Dilli), Odia and Bengali (Dhokra), and evening, Nikah and Christian weddings (Shab-e-Gul).
- **Each template has its own gate**, painted in the same style: bronze doors, glass screens, teak doors, enamel panels, rogan curtains, velvet curtains, pastel doors, a haveli gate, silver panels and balloon-silk curtains. Four pictures per gate.
- **Couple photos without boxes**, as before: the photo sits in a soft patch of green with no frame, flowers or ornaments drift over its edges, and I feather the edge when I cut it.
- **Every prompt is complete.** The style lock is inside each one: copy one block, paste it and generate.

## How to generate

1. Work on one template at a time, ideally in one chat in your image tool, so its 15 pictures match.
2. Ask for **portrait 9:16 (1080 × 1920)**. If the tool can't, generate larger and tell me; I crop.
3. Generate again if a picture has people, faces, hands, gods, religious symbols or any text or letters; if a photo page's green has a frame or border round it (say: _"remove the frame around the green; only let the flowers overlap it"_); or if the green isn't flat.
4. Check the tool's terms allow commercial use.
5. Name each file as shown under its prompt, for example `dhokra/cover.png`, and send me one folder per template. PNG or WebP are both fine; I convert to WebP, crop to 9:16 and cut the green.

## Where the files go

- Pages and photo pages: `public/suites/<template>/` as `cover`, `family`, `haldi`, `mehendi`, `sangeet`, `baraat`, `wedding`, `reception`, `reply`, `couple` and `couple-two` (`.webp`).
- Gate: `public/openings/gates/<gate>/` as `beyond`, `arch`, `door-left` and `door-right` (`.webp`). I make the fifth file, `hole.webp`, from the arch's green.
