# 15 more templates: image prompts, with depth openings and pages laid out new ways

For the owner, 23 October 2026. Fifteen more wedding templates after the 55 we have (the last ones are in `docs/TEMPLATE_PROMPTS_41_TO_55.md`). **15 pictures per template (225 in all): a depth opening of three frames, a centre picture for both names, nine pages and two couple-photo pages.** Nothing here repeats what came before: not the looks, not the kind of opening, not the page layouts and not the scenes on the pages.

## What's new this time

- **Fifteen looks we have never had.** Thewa gold on glass, Bihar's golden sikki grass, Karnataka's Hase Chittara, Chandigarh modernism, a two-ink risograph, a travel sketchbook, Bhil dot painting, spice flat-lay photography, Manipur in layered paper-cut, Cuttack silver filigree, Kerala's palm-leaf craft, mother-of-pearl inlay, modernist abstraction, black-and-white film photography with gold, and glossy 3D iridescence.
- **New places and families.** Our first templates for Bihar and Mithila, Malnad in Karnataka, Chandigarh, Manipur and Cuttack, besides Rajasthan, Madhya Pradesh, Kerala, Tamil Nadu and the big cities.
- **Depth openings: a third kind of opening.** After painted gates and tied gates, this one has no doors and nothing to untie. Three painted frames stand one behind another like a paper theatre, and the guest flies through them into the invitation. Each template has its own frames and shapes: cusped arches, coiled baskets, diamonds, a concrete wall and a round window, a flower-shaped window, a lane of arches, a dotted tree, a spice tin, paper hills, silver ovals, a leaf arch, Mughal arches, a square, circle and triangle, a colonnade, and rainbow glass. While waiting, the frames drift a little against each other as the phone tilts, so the depth shows.
- **A matching gate for every template too.** Each also gets closed doors and a tie in its own style, in `docs/TEMPLATE_GATES_56_TO_85.md` (2 more pictures per template). The gate opens the template first; the depth opening stays as a second choice.
- **Pages laid out six ways.** The words go at the top, at the bottom, in a middle band, inside a window of art, or beside a tall column of art on the left or the right. The column and window layouts are new; each template mixes at least four layouts.
- **New scenes on every page.** No more riderless horses for every baraat, tablas for every sangeet or lit palaces over a pool for every reception. Each event is shown through its template's own world: a bullock cart, a fleet of silver boats, a gateway ready to welcome the procession, a scooter and a rickshaw, palm-leaf umbrellas lining a path, a tray of sweets, a carriage on clouds.
- **Photos in new shapes.** Arches, circles, ovals, rounded squares or soft clouds, still with no frame round them.
- **Every prompt is complete.** The style lock is inside each one: copy one block, paste it and generate.

## How to generate

1. Work on one template at a time, ideally in one chat in your image tool, so its 15 pictures match. Start with the centre picture, then the three frames (front, middle, back), then the pages.
2. Ask for **portrait 9:16 (1080 × 1920)**. If the tool can't, generate larger and tell me; I crop.
3. Generate again if a picture has people, faces, hands, gods, religious symbols or any text, letters or numbers. Also generate again:
   - if the centre picture's middle field isn't empty (say: _"leave the middle field completely empty and plain"_);
   - if a frame's opening isn't one flat key colour with a crisp edge, or is the wrong shape (say: _"the opening must be one flat area of the key colour, exactly this shape, with a clean edge"_);
   - if a column page's art spreads across the calm side (say: _"keep that side completely plain; only the tall column on the other side"_);
   - if a photo page's green has a frame round it (say: _"remove the frame around the green; only let the art overlap it"_);
   - or if any key colour isn't flat.
4. Check the tool's terms allow commercial use.
5. Name each file as shown under its prompt, for example `thewa/centre.png` or `sona-parat/front.png`, and send me one folder per template. PNG or WebP are both fine.

## Where the files go

- Pages, photo pages and the centre: `public/suites/<template>/` as `centre`, `cover`, `family`, `haldi`, `mehendi`, `sangeet`, `baraat`, `wedding`, `reception`, `reply`, `couple` and `couple-two` (`.webp`).
- Opening: `public/openings/depth/<opening>/` as `front`, `middle` and `back`, with the key colour cut out of each. The opening's `beyond` is a copy of the template's centre picture.

## What I do when the pictures arrive

- Cut the key colour out of every frame, feather the photo greens, and compress everything to small WebP.
- Build depth openings as a new kind of opening, with the same name plate, countdown and buttons as now:
  - the three frames stack with the centre picture behind them;
  - while waiting, they drift a little against each other with the phone's tilt or the pointer, and each template adds its own small movement;
  - on opening, the guest flies through them one by one onto the centre picture;
  - with reduced motion, the frames fade into the centre;
  - the video's opening does the same.
- Add each centre picture as its step after the opening, as for templates 26 to 55.
- Mark every page's calm area for the words, including the new side and window layouts, set each template's lettering voice, colours, guest page look and music, and list it in the gallery under its traditions.
- Check every page, the opening, the centre, the video and the cards at 320px and 1440px, in light and dark, then put it live.
