# 15 more templates: image prompts, with bloom openings and pages laid out eight ways

For the owner, 23 October 2026. Fifteen more wedding templates after the 70 we have (the last ones are in `docs/TEMPLATE_PROMPTS_56_TO_70.md`). **15 pictures per template (225 in all): a bloom opening of three pictures, a centre picture for both names, nine pages and two couple-photo pages.** Again nothing repeats what came before: not the looks, not the kind of opening, not the scenes on the pages.

## What's new this time

- **Fifteen more looks we have never had.** Mughal carved jade, Bikaner's Usta gold relief, pixel art, black silhouettes against sunset skies, Meghalaya's living root bridges, an old Parsi theatre, Multani glazed tiles, paan in silver leaf, modern marble and brass, Goan villas with oyster-shell windows, Punjabi parandas, Assam's japi and muga silk, agate and gold, the Andaman's glowing shores, and India photographed from above.
- **New places and families.** Our first templates for Meghalaya, Assam's crafts, Goa's villas, the Andaman Islands, Multani and Saraiki families, and Bikaner, besides Punjab, Lucknow, Bombay and the big cities.
- **Bloom openings: a fourth kind of opening.** After painted gates, tied gates and depth openings, this one opens like a giant flower. Each template gives me just one outer piece, one inner piece and a heart; I copy the pieces round in rings so they cover the whole screen. On a tap, the heart lifts and the rings open outward one by one. The pieces come from each template's world, not only petals: jade petals, gold-relief lobes, pixel petals, palm fronds, fern fronds, velvet pleats, glazed tiles, betel leaves, marble inlay, oyster-shell window panes, silk tassels, japi segments, agate slices, scallop-shell ribs and wedges of fields seen from a drone.
- **A matching gate for every template too.** Each also gets closed doors and a tie in its own style, in `docs/TEMPLATE_GATES_56_TO_85.md` (2 more pictures per template). The gate opens the template first; the bloom opening stays as a second choice.
- **Pages laid out eight ways.** Two more layouts join the six from last time: art only in two opposite corners, and a slim border with all the space inside for words. Each template mixes at least five layouts.
- **New scenes again.** No baraat repeats: a jade horse's head border, a gold-relief gateway frieze, a pixel car with balloons, a carriage in silhouette, a lantern path into the forest, a wooden stage horse, a tiled doorstep with lamps, trays of paan, a Goan brass band's instruments, a red tractor with tassels, japis lining a path, an agate sunset, a seaplane at a jetty and salt pans from above.
- **A new photo shape**, the pebble, joins arches, circles, ovals, rounded squares and clouds. Still no frames round the photos.
- **Every prompt is complete.** The style lock is inside each one: copy one block, paste it and generate.

## How to generate

1. Work on one template at a time, ideally in one chat in your image tool, so its 15 pictures match. Start with the centre picture, then the outer piece, the inner piece and the heart, then the pages.
2. Ask for **portrait 9:16 (1080 × 1920)**. If the tool can't, generate larger and tell me; I crop.
3. Generate again if a picture has people, faces, hands, gods, religious symbols or any text, letters or numbers. Also generate again:
   - if the centre picture's middle field isn't empty (say: _"leave the middle field completely empty and plain"_);
   - if an outer or inner piece isn't one single piece standing upright with its narrow base at the bottom centre (say: _"just one piece, upright, its narrow base touching the bottom centre, nothing else"_);
   - if a corners page has art outside its two corners, or a border page's border is thick (say: _"keep everything else completely plain"_);
   - if a column page's art spreads across the calm side (say: _"keep that side completely plain; only the tall column on the other side"_);
   - if a photo page's green has a frame round it (say: _"remove the frame around the green; only let the art overlap it"_);
   - or if any key colour isn't flat.
4. Check the tool's terms allow commercial use.
5. Name each file as shown under its prompt, for example `yashab/centre.png` or `yashab-kamal/outer.png`, and send me one folder per template. PNG or WebP are both fine.

## Where the files go

- Pages, photo pages and the centre: `public/suites/<template>/` as `centre`, `cover`, `family`, `haldi`, `mehendi`, `sangeet`, `baraat`, `wedding`, `reception`, `reply`, `couple` and `couple-two` (`.webp`).
- Opening: `public/openings/bloom/<opening>/` as `outer`, `inner` and `heart`, with the key colour cut away. The opening's `beyond` is a copy of the template's centre picture.

## What I do when the pictures arrive

- Cut the key colour away from every piece, feather the photo greens, and compress everything to small WebP.
- Build bloom openings as a new kind of opening, with the same name plate, countdown and buttons as now:
  - a ring of outer pieces and a ring of inner pieces cover the screen, with the heart in the middle and the name plate just below it;
  - while waiting, each template moves in its own small way (a glow, a sway, a turning, a twinkle);
  - on opening, the heart lifts and the rings open outward one by one onto the centre picture;
  - with reduced motion, the flower fades into the centre;
  - the video's opening does the same.
- Add each centre picture as its step after the opening, as for templates 26 to 70.
- Mark every page's calm area for the words, including the corner and border layouts, set each template's lettering voice, colours, guest page look and music, and list it in the gallery under its traditions.
- Check every page, the opening, the centre, the video and the cards at 320px and 1440px, in light and dark, then put it live.
