# Matching gates for templates 56 to 85: image prompts

For the owner, 23 October 2026. Templates 56 to 85 (`docs/TEMPLATE_PROMPTS_56_TO_70.md` and `docs/TEMPLATE_PROMPTS_71_TO_85.md`) open with depth and bloom openings, which have no doors. Each now also gets its own gate, painted in the template's own style: closed doors held shut by a tie that the guest taps to undo. **2 pictures per template (60 in all): the closed doors and the tie.** The gate becomes each template's first opening; its depth or bloom opening stays as a second choice in the editor.

## What's new

- **Thirty new gates, one per template**, each in its template's style lock, so it matches that template's pictures.
- **Thirty new ties**, none repeating the fifteen in templates 41 to 55. They include a Thewa locket, a woven sikki fish, two painted birds, a brass medallion, a giant safety pin, a shop bell, birds carrying a garland, a string of chillies, a striped sash, a silver filigree ball, a palm-leaf knot, looped pearls, a rolling red circle, a velvet ribbon with a lily, a bubble that pops, a jade bangle, a gold sunburst, a pixel gem, two silhouette birds on a wire, a bamboo latch, a theatre's velvet rope, a brass door chain, a peacock betel-nut cutter, brass rings, a painted tile, a paranda, a gamosa knot, a quartz crystal, a sailor's knot and a kite.
- **Every prompt is complete**, with the template's style lock inside: copy one block, paste it and generate.

## How to generate

1. Make each gate in the same chat as its template's other pictures, so it matches them. Make it right after the template's centre picture, or after all 15 pictures if they are done.
2. Ask for **portrait 9:16 (1080 × 1920)**.
3. Generate again if a picture has people, faces, hands, gods, religious symbols or any text, letters or numbers. Also generate again:
   - if the two doors don't meet in one straight vertical line exactly down the middle, or something crosses that line (say: _"the two doors meet in one straight vertical line exactly down the middle, with nothing crossing it"_);
   - if there is a wall, floor or background round the doors (say: _"only the two doors, filling the whole picture to every edge"_);
   - if the tie picture shows doors or a background (say: _"only the tie, on flat key colour, nothing else"_);
   - or if the key colour isn't flat.
4. Name the files as shown under each prompt, for example `panna-pat/doors.png` and `panna-pat/tie.png`, and put them in that template's folder when you send it.

## Where the files go

- `public/openings/tied/<gate>/` as `door-left` and `door-right` (cut from the doors picture down the seam) and `tie`, with its key colour cut away. The gate's `beyond` is the template's centre picture.

## What I do when the pictures arrive

- Cut each doors picture down its seam and cut the key colour from each tie.
- Make the gate each template's first opening, built on the tied gates of templates 41 to 55: the guest taps the tie, it comes undone in its own way, and the doors open onto the centre picture.
- Keep each template's depth or bloom opening as a second choice in the editor's opening picker.
- With reduced motion, the closed doors fade into the centre; the video's opening does the same as the guest page.
