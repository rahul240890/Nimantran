# 15 new templates: image prompts, with tied-gate openings and a centre picture

For the owner, 23 October 2026. Fifteen new flower-led wedding templates after the 40 we have (`docs/TEMPLATE_PROMPTS.md`, `docs/TEMPLATE_PROMPTS_16_TO_25.md` and `docs/TEMPLATE_PROMPTS_26_TO_40.md` in the repository). **15 pictures per template (225 in all): a new tied-gate opening in three pictures, a centre picture for both names, nine pages and two couple-photo pages.** Every look, opening and centre is new: none repeats our page themes and Scenes, gates, fresh openings or the 40 templates.

## What's new this time

- **Flowers first, in fifteen new ways.** Bisque porcelain, couture organza, a botanical engraving, the Neelakurinji hills, the attar roses of Kannauj, the Valley of Flowers, palette-knife oil paint, blind-embossed paper, Bengal School wash, a silk scarf print, sheer Chanderi, sugar flowers, Paithani silk, a jamawar shawl and a palace built of flowers.
- **Tied gates: a new kind of opening.** Taken from the reels you sent, without copying any of them. The guest sees closed doors held shut by something lovely and taps it to undo it: a satin bow, a jewelled brooch, a posy on twine, an enamel butterfly, an attar bottle on a silk cord, a wild-flower garland, a silk rosette, a wax seal, shell-and-lac bangles, a gold scarf ring, a zari tassel, a sugar medallion, two gold parrots, a silver rose lock and a ball of rose petals. Each one undoes in its own way: it unties, unpins, flies off, cracks, unlocks or bursts. Then the doors swing, slide or part like curtains onto the centre picture.
- **Three pictures per opening.** The closed doors are one painting, which I cut down the seam, so both doors always match. The tie is a separate picture on a key colour, so it can move on its own. A sheet of six small pieces bursts out as it undoes.
- **A centre picture for both names.** After the doors open, the template's own centre shows the couple's names, the invitation line and the date in a large empty field: 70% of the width, from 24% to 76% of the height. It also stands behind the doors, so the opening reveals it.
- **Even more room for words.** Every page keeps more than half the picture calm: a band from 22% to 78%, or the top 60%, or the lower 55%. The 26 to 40 templates kept about half.
- **Couple photos without boxes**, as before: a soft patch of green with no frame, with flowers drifting over its edges, which I feather when I cut it.
- **Every prompt is complete.** The style lock is inside each one: copy one block, paste it and generate.

## How to generate

1. Work on one template at a time, ideally in one chat in your image tool, so its 15 pictures match. Start with the centre picture, then the doors, the tie and the pieces, then the pages.
2. Ask for **portrait 9:16 (1080 × 1920)**. If the tool can't, generate larger and tell me; I crop.
3. Generate again if a picture has people, faces, hands, gods, religious symbols or any text, letters or numbers. Also generate again:
   - if the centre picture's middle field isn't empty (say: _"leave the middle field completely empty and plain"_);
   - if the doors' seam isn't one straight line down the exact centre, or something crosses it (say: _"the two doors meet in one straight vertical line exactly down the middle, with nothing crossing it"_);
   - if the tie picture shows doors, a wall or a background (say: _"only the tie, on flat key colour, nothing else"_);
   - if a photo page's green has a frame round it (say: _"remove the frame around the green; only let the flowers overlap it"_);
   - or if any key colour isn't flat.
4. The pieces picture must show six separate small things, each alone in its own cell, solid, with no mist or glow trails.
5. Check the tool's terms allow commercial use.
6. Name each file as shown under its prompt, for example `chini-mitti/centre.png` or `reshmi-gaanth/doors.png`, and send me one folder per template. PNG or WebP are both fine.

## Where the files go

- Pages, photo pages and the centre: `public/suites/<template>/` as `centre`, `cover`, `family`, `haldi`, `mehendi`, `sangeet`, `baraat`, `wedding`, `reception`, `reply`, `couple` and `couple-two` (`.webp`).
- Opening: `public/openings/tied/<opening>/` as `door-left` and `door-right` (cut from the doors picture), `tie`, and `piece-0` to `piece-5` cut from the pieces sheet. The opening's `beyond` is a copy of the template's centre picture.

## What I do when the pictures arrive

- Cut each doors picture down its seam, cut the key colour from the ties and pieces, feather the photo greens, cut each pieces sheet into six, and compress everything to small WebP.
- Build tied gates as a new kind of opening, with the same name plate, countdown and buttons as now:
  - the doors fill the screen;
  - the tie sits on the seam just below the names, and the guest can tap it as well as the Open button;
  - it undoes in its own way, the pieces burst out, and the doors open onto the centre picture;
  - with reduced motion, the closed doors fade into the centre;
  - the video's opening does the same.
- Add each centre picture as its step after the opening, as for templates 26 to 40.
- Mark every page's calm area for the words, set each template's lettering voice, colours, guest page look and music, and list it in the gallery under its traditions.
- Check every page, the opening, the centre, the video and the cards at 320px and 1440px, in light and dark, then put it live.
