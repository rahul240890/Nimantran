# Competitors

Researched 2026-09-26. Prices change; recheck before quoting them publicly.

## Paperless Post (US)

**Offers:** three product lines — card invitations with an animated envelope, free "Flyer" event pages, greeting cards. Deep category browsing (weddings, birthdays, baby, holidays, parties, business, education, dining). Guest tools: RSVP tracking, guest messaging, co-hosts, custom questions, reminders, shareable links, scheduled sending, event detail blocks, design uploads. Designer collections (Rifle Paper Co., Oscar de la Renta, Marimekko, Martha Stewart and others). Source: [paperlesspost.com](https://www.paperlesspost.com/).

**Pricing:** coins bought in packs, 25 coins $12 to 1,000 coins $140; 2 coins per guest basic, about 6 with liner, backdrop, stamp and envelope; about $120–$145 per send for 150 guests, $300–$400+ across save-the-date, invite and thank-you. Pro plan about $250/year up to 250 guests. Free tier 50 recipients per year. Sources: [Woman Getting Married](https://www.womangettingmarried.com/paperless-post/), [Mixily](https://blog.mixily.com/paperless-post-cost-wedding/).

**Complaints:** confusing coins, add-on costs, limited print, free tier runs out across events. A competitor's page also says no custom music and no phone support ([Greenvelope](https://www.greenvelope.com/compare/greenvelope-vs-paperless-post), biased source).

### What we avoid

Coin pricing, per-guest add-on charges, a yearly free limit.

1. Interactive 3D card with music, not a flat card in an envelope.

3. One link for every function of an Indian wedding, each with its own RSVP.
4. Ten Indian languages, AI wording written natively.
5. One clear price per event.
6. Life after the event: photo album, thank-you cards, Memories gifts.
