# Video invitation templates: review and proposal

Reviewed 2026-09-27 from 16 Instagram screenshots (wedding_invitation_templates.in) and theluxuryinvites.com/shop.

## 1. What they are

There are two kinds in the screenshots.

1. **Flat illustrated videos** (Templates 15, 16, 22): mandala and elephant card, a green South Indian temple door with mango leaves, golden garden gates with a monogram, a marigold-and-lights Mehendi frame. These are vector art animated in Adobe After Effects (the "Ae" logo on the posts). The account sells the After Effects project file, so the buyer, or a designer, types in the names and renders the video.
2. **AI-painted scenes** (the "New AI wedding invitations" posts): a Ganesha shrine with "श्री गणेशाय नमः", a groom on a white horse for the baraat, a haldi scene, a beach sangeet, peacock cards on a table, a palace behind red curtains, a couple under a rose arch. These are AI images with slow camera moves, glows and text laid on top.

The Luxury Invites shop lists its videos at ₹5,999, currently on sale for ₹2,000 to ₹3,000, with a QR code and a simple RSVP website added on. Other sellers in our own notes (COMPETITORS.md) sell template videos for ₹200 to ₹500.

## 2. Why they feel impressive

- **One idea per moment.** Doors, curtains or gates open, then the blessing, then the names, then the date, each on its own beat. The guest is led through the invite like a short film instead of reading it all at once.
- **Every function gets its own scene.** Haldi is yellow with marigolds, sangeet has lights and musicians, the baraat has the horse and dhol, each with its own date and time. This is the part people remember.
- **Rich, painted backgrounds.** Palaces, flowers and warm light make it look expensive.
- **Made for WhatsApp status and Instagram.** Vertical, 30 to 60 seconds, with a popular song.

## 3. Where they are weak (and where we win)

- **It is a fixed video.** Change a time or add a function and you need a new video. No RSVP inside it, no guest list, no reminders, no second language toggle.
- **Film songs** ("Aaj Sajeya", Sonu Nigam, Arijit Singh) are used without a licence. We use our own live ragas, which is safe.
- **AI faces and sacred art are generic or wrong.** The couples are stock AI faces, not the real couple. The AI Ganesha needs a human check, and some traditions (Muslim, Sikh, Jain) don't want figures at all. Our rule stays: sacred art is commissioned and reviewed (content task C1).
- **Typos slip in.** One paid sample says "FACALITY".
- **Price.** ₹2,000 to ₹6,000 for a video alone. Our Royal pass is ₹1,999 and already includes the live 3D invite, RSVP and guest list.

## 5. Proposal: "Story mode"

Four parts, smallest and most valuable first.

**A. Story reveal (build next, right after or inside Step 12c).**
After the doors open, the invite plays in beats: blessing and sacred symbol, then the couple's names, then "together with their families", then the date, then each function as its own card with its time, then the venue, then the Reply button. Each beat fades or draws in. Guests can tap to go faster or skip. Reduced-motion phones see the finished card straight away.

- Overlap: Step 12c (being built now) adds the "opening timeline" to the engine. Story reveal is that same timeline carried on after the doors open, so the 12c work should be built to hold more than one beat.
- Size: about one step, like one of our past steps. Running cost: nothing.

**B. Function scenes for the five most-sold functions.**
Haldi, mehendi, sangeet, baraat and wedding, each with its own background, colours and particles (turmeric splash, henna drawing itself, stage lights, dhol and horse, sacred fire glow). These are already written up in MOTION.md section 3 for Step 23; this moves those five earlier.

- Size: about one step. Running cost: nothing.

**C. Video export for WhatsApp status (Step 21, moved earlier).**
The same story played into a vertical MP4, 30 to 45 seconds, with our own raga, made on the host's phone or computer so we pay no server cost. PRICING.md already has "extra MP4 ₹299", so this becomes a paid extra soon after payments go live.

- Size: about one step. Running cost: close to nothing if made in the browser; if older phones can't render it, a server render costs a few rupees per video (estimate, to confirm when built).

**D. Painted "Signature" scenes (after launch).**
A small library of rich painted backgrounds (palace courtyard, marigold mandap, beach sangeet, haldi garden) for a premium collection. We make these once, not per customer: generated or commissioned, then cleaned up and checked by an artist. No deities and no faces from AI; deity art stays commissioned (C1). Per-couple portraits stay with Step 20 (AI couple art from their own photos).

- Cost: generating images is cheap (a few rupees each, inferred); the real cost is artist clean-up and review time. Painted images are heavy, so they load only on capable phones, with our vector card as the fallback.
